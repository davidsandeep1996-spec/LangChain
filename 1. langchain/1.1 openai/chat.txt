⚠️ What to Double-Check in Person
Since the bike is resprayed & cheap, do a quick inspection before saying yes:
Frame – Look around joints (bottom bracket, seat tube, head tube) for cracks. A respray can hide issues.
Wheels – Spin them; do they wobble side-to-side? If badly bent, costly to fix.
Brakes – Squeeze hard. Does the bike stop firmly?
Gears – Shift through a few while riding. Do they change smoothly?
Chain – Check for rust. A rusty but usable chain is okay at this price, replacement is ~£10–15.
Test ride – Sit, pedal, and listen for strange noises (clunks, grinding).
🛠️ Immediate Fixes You’ll Need
Saddle replacement (~£20).
Basic clean & lube (~£5–10 DIY).
Lights + lock (~£40–50 if you don’t have).
🚀 Bottom Line
For £20 this is 100% worth it if it rides okay and frame is solid.
You’ll have a functional commuter for a fraction of the usual price — just expect to spend a little extra making it comfy & safe.
